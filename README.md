## How to Install as an App

### On iPhone/iPad:
1. Click the link above
2. Tap the **Share** button (bottom)
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**
5. The app appears on your home screen!

### On Android:
1. Click the link above
2. Tap the **"Install"** button
3. The app appears on your home screen!

### On Computer:
1. Click the link above
2. Click the **install icon** in your address bar
3. The app appears in your applications!
